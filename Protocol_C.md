# Protocol_C: complete independent mathematical review

Framework edition 1 | November 9, 2026

## Completion standard

Obtain non-author mathematical review of the complete argument for every announced result, including external theorem applications, substantive internal steps, connecting arguments and essential computations. Finish with independent review of the assembled proof and a reproducible research release.

This framework uses an author-maintained inventory and a complete review-coverage map. It does not require a second, inventory-blind reconstruction followed by a separate reconciliation process. Reviewers must nevertheless read the actual entire argument and report missing inputs. Checking only the entries already present in a ledger does not meet Protocol_C.

The standard remains informal review modulo published external theorems. It is neither a formal proof nor a guarantee that reviewers will find every error. No known necessary mathematical gap or required source check may remain open in a completion claim. Report a genuinely conditional theorem with its conditions intact.

## Fix claims and evidence

Record target statements, objects, quantifiers, bounds, representations, deliverables, release destinations and authorizations in `PROJECT.md`. Give claims, sources, uses, computations, findings and decisions stable identifiers. Use one authoritative ledger and link to proofs rather than repeating them.

The author traces the claims in the title, abstract, main results and corollaries through the manuscript, appendices and linked proofs. Include substantive uncited inputs and existence, uniformity and effectivity assertions. Each required step needs a proved internal supplier, a qualified published theorem or a justified computation.

Bind records and decisions to immutable revisions and file identities. For applications, include the supplying statement, proof or source location, actual construction and consuming context. Record exact editions and corrections. Check final published statements and definitions; an unchecked comparison with an author version remains open. An unpublished dependency needs a project proof or an explicit condition in the claimed theorem.

Published inputs are trusted after these application checks unless a concrete concern requires investigation. Reconstruct their relevant proof passages to resolve ambiguity or a reported defect, not automatically every ancestor theorem in the literature.

## Review the complete argument

Assign non-author reviewers coherent units covering the entire proof. The coverage map lists the complete set of proof-bearing sections, appendices, linked arguments and computational claims, their reviewers, exact versions and decisions. Explain exclusions such as background discussion. A reviewer confirms that this map covers the sources supporting every announced result.

Reviewers may use the existing inventory throughout. They read the proof itself and test its inferences, identify omissions, and add missing applications. No separate source-first table or blind-reconstruction workflow is mandatory.

For each substantive application, check:

1. The actual source hypotheses, definitions, quantifiers and permitted parameters.
2. The constructed object, its domain and the coverage of the required family.
3. The maps and identifications connecting the supplied result to the next step.
4. Coefficient rings, integral structures, normalization, signs, duals and local conventions where relevant.
5. Exceptional cases, degeneracies, local conditions and endpoint behavior.
6. Uniformity, limit order, estimates after normalization, and simultaneous parameter or witness choices.
7. The precise conclusion used downstream and its strength relative to what was proved.

Give the reasoning in the manuscript or linked argument. A familiar theorem name or a nonempty checklist cell is not evidence of a valid transition. Check representative adverse cases when useful, without mistaking examples for a proof.

For probability or analysis, examine the actual information available, stopping rules, laws, integrability, localization and error after division. In algebra or geometry, examine integral versus rational conclusions, local-to-global steps, boundary extension and identity of constructed objects. Apply checks to the actual mathematics rather than copying irrelevant subject checklists.

## Independence and findings

Record each reviewer's identity, human or model status, task, tools, prior exposure, reviewed versions and scope. Authors cannot independently accept their own proof or repair. If a reviewer supplies a substantive repair, obtain another non-author decision. Distinct agents may make correlated mistakes; agreement is not a proof.

Register every received audit and substantive objection. Preserve unfavorable findings. Resolve each by an independently checked repair, a supported rebuttal, or an explicit, legitimate change in the claimed theorem. Track historical justification separately from its incorporation in the current manuscript. Incomplete review and invalid inference are different diagnoses.

After an error, assess affected consumers and plausible repeated uses of the same defective reasoning. Correct current summaries and repeated assertions. Record the extent of that check and remaining questions in the existing finding. Preserve original evidence and superseded decisions.

Reviews state accepted within scope, changes required, incomplete review or rejected. Do not substitute consistency checking for reconstruction of a substantive argument. Reuse accepted units at unchanged contracts and versions. A changed dependency can reopen an unchanged paragraph; a harmless relocation does not require reproving it.

## Constructions and computations

Define all objects and constants unambiguously and prove required existence and termination. State whether each supplied artifact is a full expansion, symbolic expression, compressed encoding, generator or certificate.

For every essential computation, independently review the specification and the checker's mathematical justification, and inspect its implementation or establish the stated trust in a standard implementation. Check both directions of a claimed equivalence, exceptional branches and the link to the final theorem as applicable.

Preserve input files, code, certificates, dependency versions, commands, results and hashes. Separate production from checking where feasible; include meaningful negative tests. Reproduce essential checks from a fresh copy of the selected release files. Report platform restrictions and external trust honestly. An external certificate described as an axiom remains an assumption in a formalization.

## Closure and release

After the last substantive repair, a non-author reviewer checks the assembled argument from every announced claim through its suppliers. Accepted unit reviews may be reused, but their composition, shared conventions and simultaneous choices must be assessed. The reviewer also confirms complete coverage against the actual proof sources. These decisions may share one report.

Completion requires current acceptance for all required units and their assembly, resolved findings, qualified external inputs, explicit constructions, reproduced computations and incorporation of all accepted repairs. If closure produces a new repair, review the affected work and update the assembly decision. Additional rounds without a changed input or concrete question are unnecessary.

Synchronize the manuscript and supplied PDF, README, source and application inventories, findings ledger, artifact catalog and cumulative handoff for each research release. Preserve relevant research history, negative results, proofs, reviews and corrections with provenance and status. Exclude conversations, credentials and unrelated private material; identify unavailable or restricted evidence without hiding its effect.

Record the version, date, source revision, artifact identities, reproduction commands, reviewer decisions and limitations. Preserve earlier releases, check navigation links, and read back every authorized publication destination against the manifest. State why release-only tasks are deferred for a small non-release change.

Report completion as **Protocol_C complete independent informal review modulo published theorems**. Record-checking software can detect inconsistencies in recorded evidence, not certify the mathematics or discover every missing dependency.
