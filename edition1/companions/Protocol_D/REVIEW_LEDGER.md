## Application obligations and related uses

For each substantive application, link evidence for the nine obligations in PROTOCOL.md: hypotheses; actual objects; domain/index coverage; transition maps; identity of the consumed object; coefficients and normalizations; local conditions; exceptional cases; downstream consumption. State what is needed, what is constructed, why they match and where that is proved. Give reasons for nonapplicability.

Record exact proposed edits, available-versus-needed conclusions, connecting derivations, adverse cases and affected consumers. After a substantive failure, record the bounded related-use search, candidate occurrences, decisions and independent coverage acknowledgment. Check repeated current assertions for rejected claims.

Keep application, coverage and final-assembly decisions distinct in scope. They may share one ledger and eligible reviewer. List structural checks and required negative controls separately from mathematical review. Empty fields, favorable status labels and passing checks do not establish proof or coverage.
